CONCEPT & DATA INTEGRITY

Modeled on portable electronic devotional chanting boxes (念佛机). Inside, the device will house physical sculptural forms corresponding to empirical acoustic measurements from sacred chant traditions (Tibetan Buddhist, Gregorian, Dhikr, Hindu, Shamanic).

Framing: The prayer machine is an artistic lens for perceptual listening, not a claim that all chants originate from prayer machines. Acoustic metrics describe excerpts as wholes without fabricated contours.
